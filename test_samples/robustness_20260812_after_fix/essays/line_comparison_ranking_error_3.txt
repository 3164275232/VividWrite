The graph traces daily passenger numbers for three public transport modes over the decade from 2010 to 2020.

Overall, Rail was the lowest transport mode in 2018, while the ordering differed at other observations.

In 2010, Metro served 0.8 million passengers, compared with 1.9 million in 2020. At the outset, Rail carried 1.1 million passengers and ultimately reached 2.2 million. In 2010, Bus served 1.8 million passengers, compared with 1.3 million in 2020. The Bus figure in 2018 was 1.5 million. At the 2018 observation, Rail stood at 2.0 million. At the 2018 observation, Metro stood at 1.7 million.

The selected middle year helps indicate the position of each service between the first and final observations.
