The circular chart presents how household expenditure in Canada was allocated among six items in 2024.

Overall, Other was the largest category in the household budget, with unequal shares across the other items.

The proportion spent on Leisure was 12%. A share of 10% went to Utilities. Transport accounted for 17% of expenditure. A share of 32% went to Housing. Households allocated 21% of their spending to Food. Households allocated 8% of their spending to Other.

These proportions show the different amounts of attention given to the reported spending items.
