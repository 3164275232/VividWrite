The circular chart presents how household expenditure in Canada was allocated among six items in 2024.

Overall, Leisure decreased over the period, while the six spending categories occupied unequal shares.

The proportion spent on Food was 21%. The proportion spent on Transport was 17%. The proportion spent on Leisure was 12%. The proportion spent on Utilities was 10%. A share of 32% went to Housing. Other accounted for 8% of expenditure.

These proportions show the different amounts of attention given to the reported spending items.
