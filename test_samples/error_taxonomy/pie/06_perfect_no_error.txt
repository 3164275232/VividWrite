The pie chart illustrates how average household expenditure in Canada was distributed across six categories in 2024.

Overall, housing accounted for the largest share of household spending, while other expenditure represented the smallest proportion. Food and transport were the next two major categories.

Housing made up 32% of the average household budget. Food was the second-largest expense at 21%, followed by transport at 17%. Together, these three categories represented 70% of total expenditure.

The remaining categories each accounted for a considerably smaller share. Leisure represented 12% of spending, while utilities made up 10%. Other expenditure was the smallest category, at 8%.

Housing exceeded food by 11 percentage points and transport by 15 points. Food was 4 points higher than transport, while leisure was 2 points above utilities. The difference between utilities and other expenditure was also 2 percentage points. As the chart reports a single year, it presents a distribution rather than a change over time.
