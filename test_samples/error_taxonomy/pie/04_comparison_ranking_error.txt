The pie chart compares the proportions of average household expenditure assigned to six spending categories in Canada in 2024.

Overall, food was presented as the dominant category, while other expenditure represented the smallest part of the budget. Housing and transport also accounted for substantial proportions.

Housing made up 32% of household spending, and food accounted for 21%. Transport represented 17%, followed by leisure at 12% and utilities at 10%. Other expenditure had the lowest stated share, at 8%.

Food occupied the highest position among the six categories. It was 4 percentage points above transport, while housing exceeded food by 11 points. Leisure and utilities were separated by only 2 percentage points, and the difference between utilities and other spending was also 2 points.
