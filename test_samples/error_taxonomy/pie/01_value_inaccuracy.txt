The pie chart illustrates how average household expenditure in Canada was distributed among six categories in 2024.

Overall, housing represented the largest part of the household budget, while the remaining categories each accounted for considerably smaller proportions. Essential expenses formed a substantial share of total spending.

Housing made up 32% of expenditure, followed by food at 21%. Transport was the third-largest category, accounting for 17%, while leisure represented 12%.

Utilities comprised 14% of the average budget. The final category, other expenditure, accounted for 8%. Housing was therefore 11 percentage points above food and 15 points higher than transport. Leisure exceeded other spending by 4 points, and the gap between utilities and the miscellaneous category was 6 percentage points.
