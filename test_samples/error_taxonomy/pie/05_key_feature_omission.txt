The pie chart illustrates the distribution of average Canadian household expenditure across spending categories in 2024.

Overall, housing took the greatest share of the household budget. Food and transport formed the next two major expenses, while leisure and utilities accounted for smaller proportions.

Housing represented 32% of total expenditure, which was 11 percentage points more than food at 21%. Transport made up 17%, placing it 4 points below food. Leisure accounted for 12% of spending, while utilities represented 10%.

The gap between housing and transport was 15 percentage points. Food and transport together comprised 38% of the budget, and leisure plus utilities formed a further 22%. These figures show that accommodation costs were the dominant expense among the categories described.
