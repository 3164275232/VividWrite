The pie chart presents the percentage distribution of average household spending in Canada across six categories in 2024.

Overall, housing occupied the largest share of the budget. The other categories were smaller, although food and transport still represented substantial household expenses.

Housing accounted for 32% of total expenditure. Food represented 17%, whereas transport made up 21%. Leisure and utilities were responsible for 12% and 10% respectively, and other spending formed the remaining 8%.

The difference between housing and food was 15 percentage points, while housing exceeded transport by 11 points. Leisure was 2 percentage points above utilities and 4 points higher than the other category. Together, the three smaller categories of leisure, utilities and other expenditure comprised 30% of the household budget.
