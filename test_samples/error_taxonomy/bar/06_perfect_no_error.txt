The bar chart compares household recycling rates in five UK cities in 2015 and 2020, measured as percentages.

Overall, every city recorded a higher recycling rate in 2020 than in 2015. Bristol had the highest figure in both years, whereas Liverpool remained the lowest. Manchester experienced the largest increase over the period.

In 2015, 42% of households in Bristol recycled their waste. Sheffield ranked second at 38%, followed by Leeds at 35%. The corresponding rates in Manchester and Liverpool were lower, at 31% and 28% respectively.

By 2020, Bristol's figure had risen to 55%, while Sheffield and Leeds reached 51% and 48%. Manchester increased more sharply, from 31% to 46%, although it remained below Leeds. Liverpool also improved, climbing from 28% to 39%.

The increases in Bristol, Leeds and Sheffield were all 13 percentage points. Manchester recorded a larger rise of 15 points, while Liverpool's increase was 11 points. Despite these changes, the ranking of the five cities was unchanged in 2020.
