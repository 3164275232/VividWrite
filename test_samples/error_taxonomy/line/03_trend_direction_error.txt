The line graph compares average daily use of three public transport modes between 2010 and 2020, with passenger numbers given in millions.

Overall, all three forms of transport increased over the period. Rail recorded the largest final figure, while bus remained an important mode throughout the years shown.

Bus use increased from 1.8 million passengers per day in 2010 to 1.3 million in 2020. Rail, meanwhile, rose from 1.1 million in 2010 to 2.2 million in 2020. Metro followed the same upward direction, growing from 0.8 million in 2010 to 1.9 million in 2020.

By 2020, rail carried more passengers than metro, and the metro figure was above the number using buses. The strongest growth therefore occurred in rail use, although metro also experienced a substantial rise over the ten-year period.
