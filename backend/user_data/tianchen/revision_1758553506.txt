The chart illustrates the trends in telephone call categories in Australia from 2001 to 2008, encompassing local fixed line calls, national and international fixed line calls, and mobile calls. 

Overall, there was a noticeable decline in local fixed line calls, while both national and international fixed line calls and mobile calls exhibited an upward trajectory throughout the period. 

In 2001, local fixed line calls numbered 73 billion minutes, peaking at 90 billion minutes in 2005 before experiencing a decline to 73 billion minutes by 2008. Conversely, national and international fixed line calls increased steadily from 38 billion minutes in 2001 to 58 billion minutes in 2008, with a particularly marked rise in the final years of the period. Mobile calls, which started at a modest 3 billion minutes in 2001, saw significant growth, reaching 48 billion minutes by 2008. This indicates a shift in communication preferences, with a clear movement away from traditional fixed line calls towards mobile communication.
